# Business Questions – Climate & Crop Yield Analysis

## Dashboard Objective

To analyze how climate factors such as rainfall, temperature, and humidity vary across time, seasons, locations, and crops, and how these factors relate to agricultural yield.

## Rainfall Analysis

1. How has average rainfall changed over the years?
2. Which seasons receive the highest and lowest rainfall?
3. How does rainfall vary across crops?
4. Which locations experience higher rainfall?

## Temperature Analysis

5. How does average temperature vary year over year?
6. What seasonal temperature patterns are observed?
7. How does temperature vary across crops?
8. How does temperature differ by location?

## Humidity Analysis

9. How has average humidity changed over time?
10. Which seasons show higher humidity?
11. How does humidity vary across crops?
12. Which locations experience higher humidity?

## Yield Analysis

13. How does crop yield vary across years?
14. Are there seasonal yield patterns?
15. Which crops have higher yields?
16. How does yield vary across locations?
